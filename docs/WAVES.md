# Three-wave job release

Waves are off unless an admin turns on **Waves enabled** in Settings. While that setting is off, every open request is visible to every eligible driver immediately. There is no wave delay and no invitation limit. Matched for You is coverage. All Georgia Requests is every other eligible open job. Wave release applies to both sections when waves are on. A job can still have only five active offers at a time. A driver can offer on more than one job.

While the setting is on, visibility is calculated from the time the request became `open`. A missed cron cannot hide or reveal a job. Wave 2 and wave 3 notifications are not sent by a five-minute schedule. Vercel Hobby cannot run that often. The feed, offers, and claims read the same timestamps.

An admin invitation releases that driver immediately and is audited.

## Who counts

A matching driver is not removed, not blocked by the customer, and either Online or Offline with **Job alerts while Offline** on. That setting defaults to on. Counting only people who are Online does not exhaust a wave.

## Wave 1

Immediately. The nearest drivers whose Areas I cover match the pickup, up to 10.

## Wave 2

Ten minutes after the request opens, or immediately when fewer than 10 drivers match Areas I cover. The next drivers within 50 miles of the pickup, up to 20 more. Wave 1 drivers are not counted again.

## Wave 3

Twenty minutes after the request opens, or at the Wave 2 time when fewer than 20 further drivers are within 50 miles. Every other eligible driver in Georgia can see the request. Wave 3 does not send a push to every driver in Georgia.

## Alerts while waves are off

When a request opens, Online drivers whose Areas I cover match get a push. Offline drivers with Job alerts while Offline on get one too, when their areas match. Drivers outside those areas still see the request in the feed. They do not get that push.
